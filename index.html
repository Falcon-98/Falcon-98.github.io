What is Falcon 98?

Falcon 98 is a software and technology services company based in Pallewela, Gampaha District, Sri Lanka. It builds custom software, websites, web applications, mobile apps, cloud solutions, AI and data analytics systems and cybersecurity tools for businesses in Sri Lanka and around the world.

Why is Falcon 98 a strong choice for software development in Sri Lanka?

Falcon 98 combines end-to-end engineering (web, mobile, cloud, AI and security) with a portfolio of live, publicly accessible products such as Falcon Forge, MeetAcross and the Blue Team Cyber Dashboard. Clients work directly with the engineers, get transparent pricing and receive SEO-ready, secure and scalable software.

What software solutions does Falcon 98 provide?

Falcon 98 provides custom software development, web development and web design, e-commerce, mobile app development for Android and iOS, cloud migration and DevOps, AI and generative AI integration, data analytics and business intelligence, cybersecurity assessments and IT consulting.

Where is Falcon 98 located?

Falcon 98 is located at No. 39/2, Nungamuwa, Pallewela 11150, Sri Lanka, close to Colombo and Gampaha. The team serves clients across Sri Lanka, including Colombo, Gampaha, Kandy, Negombo and Galle, and works remotely with international clients.

Does Falcon 98 work with international clients?

Yes. Falcon 98 works with clients worldwide, offering offshore software development from Sri Lanka with flexible meeting times across time zones, communication in English and online project management.

How much does software or website development cost at Falcon 98?

Pricing depends on the scope, features and timeline of each project. Falcon 98 provides a free consultation and a transparent, itemised quotation in LKR or USD. Contact info@falcon98.com, call +94 78 446 4128 or message on WhatsApp to get a quote.

How do I contact Falcon 98?

You can email info@falcon98.com, call +94 78 446 4128 or +94 11 340 1721, chat on WhatsApp at +94 78 446 4128, or book a meeting online. Office hours are Monday to Friday 9 AM–6 PM and Saturday 10 AM–4 PM (Sri Lanka time).
